Reformation Run

Race down the road from Wittenberg to Dort. Seventy-five questions on the Protestant Reformation and Reformed theology stand between you and the finish.

  • ReadEach question appears at the top while you run.
  • ChooseFour answers wait at the end of the lanes. Read them early, switch to the true one, then press the right arrow to speed-run it.
  • SurviveWrong gates cost a heart. Four right in a row earns one back.
  • BowlCardinals (red hats) are Rome's senior church officials who opposed Luther, so dodge them. Run into popes to knock them flying for bonus points.
